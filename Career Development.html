Role Models

Pascal, now retired, spent his career at Airbus.

About him His path from a working class background to engineering studies in Toulouse, a PhD in system safety, and research in California shows the value of perseverance and lifelong learning. Although he faced setbacks, including a failed interview and a lack of confidence sometimes, he eventually found his place in the aerospace industry.
Career journey At Airbus, he contributed to flight control systems and innovation for the A380, taking on roles as technical expert, systems architect, project manager, and department head. He valued thoughtful career choices, openness to opportunities, and developing his team.
What he taught me Engineering takes sound judgment, teamwork, adaptability, and the confidence to speak up. His career inspires me to pursue embedded systems in the space sector with curiosity and a commitment to meaningful work.

Didier built an inspiring career in critical embedded systems across several industries.

About him He began as an electronics technician, then continued his education and became an engineer. His journey shows that it is possible to grow professionally while continuing to learn.
Career journey Didier progressed from development engineering to architecture, technical and project leadership, and eventually technical director. He worked in aerospace, rail, defence, and industry, contributing to critical embedded systems.
What he taught me His path shows me that a career does not have to be linear. In a smaller organisation, contributing to research and development, manufacturing, and customer support can offer a broad view of how reliable systems are designed and delivered. His curiosity and interest in innovation encourage me to keep learning and stay open to new opportunities.

Cinthya is a real-time systems engineer specialising in software testing and validation for the railway industry.

About her After studying physics with a focus on electronics, electrical energy and automation, she joined ENSEEIHT in the Communicating Systems programme. She then gained experience in embedded systems development before moving into software testing and validation.
Career journey In her current work, she checks that railway control software meets customer requirements and demanding safety and reliability standards. Her experience connects embedded development with the verification needed to operate critical real time systems safely.
What she taught me Cinthya's path shows me that testing and validation are central engineering disciplines, not just final checks. Her experience at ENSEEIHT and in safety critical rail systems is especially relevant to my goal of designing dependable embedded systems for space applications.
