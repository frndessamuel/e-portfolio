International Mobility

Choice 1 — Italy

Italy, particularly the Politecnico di Torino, is an appealing choice because of its strong industrial links and engineering programs. I would like to study computer or electronic engineering, focusing on embedded software, control systems and connected vehicles. A practical project would let me apply these ideas to a real engineering challenge. I would coordinate my course choices with ENSEEIHT to ensure that the exchange fits my degree, while also discovering Italian culture and developing my ability to collaborate in an international environment.

Choice 2 — Canada

Canada, particularly Montréal, interests me for its strong computer and electrical engineering programs. At Polytechnique Montréal, I would look for courses in embedded software, operating systems and industrial computing, and apply what I learn in a practical team project. I would prepare my course plan with ENSEEIHT so that the exchange contributes to my degree. Studying in Montréal would also let me experience a bilingual, international environment and strengthen my technical communication and collaboration skills.

Choice 3 — United Kingdom

I am interested in the United Kingdom, particularly the University of Bristol or the University of Southampton, for their strengths in electronics and computer science. I would like to study hardware-software co-design, real-time systems and software for connected devices. A team project would give me the opportunity to develop and test a system while learning how engineering students approach critical applications. I would agree on a suitable course plan with ENSEEIHT and build confidence in English through lectures, presentations and group work.

An international mobility program would allow me to deepen my knowledge of computer and electronic engineering in a new academic environment. I hope to take courses that complement my studies at ENSEEIHT, work on a practical project with students from different backgrounds, and discover new approaches to solving engineering problems. I would also like to improve my English through lectures, presentations and everyday interactions, while learning about the host country's culture and way of life. My objective is to return with broader technical and personal perspectives that I can apply to my studies and future career.
